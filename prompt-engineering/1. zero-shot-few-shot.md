# Zero-Shot and Few-Shot Prompting

## 1. Zero-Shot Prompt

**Prompt:**

Classify the following customer complaint as either Urgent or Not Urgent.

A complaint is Urgent if it involves a serious issue requiring immediate attention. Otherwise, classify it as Not Urgent.

Customer complaint: {complaint}

## 2. Few-Shot Prompt

**Prompt:**

Classify each customer complaint as Urgent or Not Urgent.

**Examples:**

Complaint: My payment was deducted twice.

Classification: Urgent

Complaint: I want to know how to update my profile picture.

Classification: Not Urgent

Complaint: The product overheated and caused a burn.

Classification: Urgent

Now classify this complaint:

Customer complaint: {complaint}

Classification:

## Difference

Zero-shot prompting gives instructions without examples whereas Few-shot prompting provides examples to demonstrate the expected classification before classifying a new complaint.
