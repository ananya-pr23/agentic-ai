# Failure and Re-planning

## Test Case

- Destination: Tokyo
- Days: 5
- Total Budget: ₹30,000
- Initial Daily Budget: ₹8,000
- Interests: Food, culture and sightseeing

## Initial Plan

The initial daily budget was ₹8,000.

Estimated cost:

₹8,000 × 5 = ₹40,000

The estimated cost exceeded the user's total budget of ₹30,000.

## Agent Observation

The agent detected that the initial plan was over budget.

## Re-planning

The agent recalculated the daily budget:

₹30,000 ÷ 5 = ₹6,000 per day

The plan was then checked again against the total budget.

## Result

- Initial Estimated Cost: ₹40,000
- Revised Estimated Cost: ₹30,000
- Re-planned: Yes
- Final Budget Status: Within budget

This demonstrates the agent's ability to observe a failed condition and re-plan instead of simply returning the original plan.
